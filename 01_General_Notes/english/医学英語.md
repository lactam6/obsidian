# 1. Small Talk
- A. Hi. How are you doing?
	- (more polite): How are you?
	- (more casual): How's it going?
- B. I'm pretty good. How about you?
	- I'm great/super/fantastic
	- I'm pretty good/OK/not bad/doing all right = fine
	- I'm tired/sleepy/stressed out
	- I'm hungry/thirsty
	- I'm sick/ I have a fever
- A. I'm OK. What's new?
- B. Oh. last weekend I had a hanami with my friends from the volleyball club. How about you?
- A. Nothing much. I had to finish a paper, so I spent the weekend at my desk.
- B. Oh, that's too bad.
	- I'm sorry to head that
	- (for good news) Sounds great / sounds good.
